The Game

The fastest-growing
sport on earth.
Coming to your city.

Padel is tennis meets squash. Played in doubles on a glass-walled court, scored like tennis, but with the walls in play. The court is smaller, the racket is solid, the rallies last longer — and the learning curve is almost vertical.

You can pick it up in a single session. You will lose your evenings to it within a month. It's the most addictive racket sport on the planet, and Birmingham is about to find out why.
