AI On Every Court

The court is
watching.
In a good way.

Every one of our six courts is fitted with AI technology. It follows the ball, reads the play and turns your session into something you can actually watch back, learn from and send to the group chat.

01

Tracks your game

Cameras follow every rally from the first serve to match point. Nothing is missed, nothing needs setting up — walk on and play.

02

Analyses your shots

Shot-by-shot breakdown of how you're actually playing, so you know what to work on before your next session.

03

Instant replay

That winner down the line, on screen, seconds later. Settle the point and take the credit.

04

Fair play, guaranteed

Line calls and scoring backed by the footage. No arguments, no re-serves, no "that was out".

05

Download & share

Your full match, yours to keep. Download it, clip it, post it — every session leaves with you.
